About QuickNotes

Quicknotes is a simple web app for writing down in few words your ideas that come in mind.It helps keep your ideas in one list so that they can be referenced and reviewed later.

How to use QuickNotes.

  1. Open the QuickNote Home page
  2. Type your note in the "New note" box .
  3. In few words type what you want to check later.
  4. Click the add note button to save your note .
  5. Review your notes under "Your notes".

Features

  • Add a note in seconds
  • See all your notes in one list
  • Live note counter
  • Simple, distraction free layout

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add a note
Tab Move to the next field
